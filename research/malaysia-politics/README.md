# research/malaysia-politics/

Research briefs on Malaysian federal and state politics.

**Use for:** parties and coalitions, Parliament and Hansard, bills and Acts, elections and by-elections, state governments, Cabinet decisions, court proceedings, MACC and integrity matters.

**Not for:** budget numbers (`public-finance/`) or foreign policy (`geopolitics/`).

**Skill:** `malaysian-political-research` · **Template:** `templates/research-brief.md` · **File name:** `YYYY-MM-DD-topic-slug.md`

**Key primary sources:** parlimen.gov.my (Hansard, bills), lom.agc.gov.my (laws), spr.gov.my (results), state assembly records, court judgments.
