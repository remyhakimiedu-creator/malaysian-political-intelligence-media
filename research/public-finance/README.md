**Use for:** Budget documents, Fiscal Outlook, revenue and expenditure, deficit and debt, guarantees, subsidies and targeted aid, taxes, Auditor-General findings.

**Not for:** general macroeconomic data such as GDP or inflation (`economy/`).

**Skill:** `malaysian-political-research` + `public-finance-analysis` · **Template:** `templates/research-brief.md` · **File name:** `YYYY-MM-DD-topic-slug.md`

**Key primary sources:** MOF Budget and Fiscal Outlook documents, Federal Government Financial Statements, Auditor-General's Reports, PAC reports.
