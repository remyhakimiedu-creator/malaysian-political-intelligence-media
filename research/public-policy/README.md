# research/public-policy/

Research briefs on sector policies and their effects.

**Use for:** education, health, housing, transport, digital and AI policy, environment and energy transition, social protection, labour rules, regulatory changes.

**Not for:** the political fight over a policy (`malaysia-politics/`) or its fiscal cost (`public-finance/`) — cross-link instead.

**Skill:** `malaysian-political-research` · **Template:** `templates/research-brief.md` · **File name:** `YYYY-MM-DD-topic-slug.md`

**Key primary sources:** ministry policy documents and blueprints, Gazette notifications, Hansard answers, DOSM data, Auditor-General findings.
