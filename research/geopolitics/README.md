# research/geopolitics/

Research briefs on Malaysia's international position.

**Use for:** foreign policy statements, ASEAN, South China Sea and maritime issues, US–China competition, bilateral relations, trade agreements, defence, UN positions.

**Not for:** domestic politics (`malaysia-politics/`).

**Skill:** `malaysian-political-research` + `geopolitical-analysis` · **Template:** `templates/research-brief.md` · **File name:** `YYYY-MM-DD-topic-slug.md`

**Key primary sources:** Wisma Putra (kln.gov.my), ASEAN Secretariat, official statements of every government involved, UN records. Use sources from more than one country.
