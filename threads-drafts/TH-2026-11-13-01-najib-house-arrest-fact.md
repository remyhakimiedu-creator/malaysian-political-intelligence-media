# TH-2026-11-13-01 — Najib house arrest: the short facts

## Posts

<!-- post -->
Najib Razak began serving the rest of his SRC International sentence under house arrest on 10 Nov 2026.

• Basis: a conditional royal pardon (18 Sep 2026). The condition, a RM50 million fine, was paid on 6 Nov.
• Where: his Kuala Lumpur home, until 23 Aug 2028
• Then: back to Kajang Prison on 24 Aug 2028 for his separate 15-year 1MDB sentence (appeal pending)

Sources: Bernama, NST
