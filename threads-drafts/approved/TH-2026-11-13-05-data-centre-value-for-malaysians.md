# TH-2026-11-13-05 — Do Malaysians get enough from data centres?

## Posts

<!-- post -->
Do Malaysians get enough from the data-centre boom?

• 27 approved projects worth RM225.97 billion are expected to create 4,053 jobs: ~RM55.8 million of investment per job
• Bank Negara estimates the sector's direct contribution at ~1% of real GDP (2025 forecast)
• Treasury's top civil servant: "we perhaps overdid the red carpet"

The jobs pay well. The open questions are scale, spillovers and the tax cost of incentives.

Sources: Parliament; BNM; The Edge
