# TH-2026-11-13-03 — Approved isn't built

## Posts

<!-- post -->
Malaysia's data-centre boom in 3 numbers:

• RM225.97 billion approved for 27 Task Force projects. Only 1 was operating (Digital Ministry reply to Parliament, Oct 2026)
• 5.65 GW of power reserved by data centres already on TNB's grid
• ~1.26 GW actually drawn (June 2026)

Approved isn't built. Reserved isn't used.

That gap is why the real test is what gets built, and what it uses.

Sources: Parliament written reply; TNB analyst briefing
