2. **Selangor reserve margin and 632 MLD projection**: conflicting secondary figures, and the primary page returned 403. Verify with Air Selangor or SPAN.
3. **PM's 24 Feb 2026 statement** stopping non-AI data-centre applications: secondary sources only. Check Hansard.
4. **Google US$15 billion plan**: search result only.
5. **Energy Commission's "85% of households protected"**: reported, not checked against the EC's primary text.
6. **Actual vs reserved power (22%)**: derived by a third party from one month of TNB data. Indicative only.
